模型资源说明:
model/ 中的动作数据由项目方在物理仿真中生成, 不包含任何第三方动作捕捉的原始帧.
生成过程中参考了以下公开动作捕捉数据, 在此致谢并署名:
- LAFAN1 (Ubisoft La Forge, Harvey et al. 2020, "Robust Motion In-betweening"), https://github.com/ubisoft/ubisoft-laforge-animation-dataset, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0
- ACCAD Motion Capture Library (The Ohio State University, Advanced Computing Center for the Arts and Design), https://accad.osu.edu/research/motion-lab/mocap-system-and-data, CC BY 3.0
初始站姿为项目方手工设定的关节角度.
